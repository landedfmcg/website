About Landed

The commercial team, on your side of the desk.

Inside the big consumer goods suppliers, growing sales in retail is an industrial process. There are category analysts reading the scan data, revenue managers modelling every promotion before it runs, and account managers who negotiate for a living. When one of them walks into Coles or Woolworths, an entire commercial machine walks in behind them.

Landed comes from inside that machine. We are senior commercial operators who have spent fifteen years on the supplier side of Australia's toughest retailers, across household brands and hundreds of negotiations. We have won the listings, run the accounts, defended the ranges and fought the margin battles that decide whether a brand grows or quietly disappears.

Most brands do not lose on the product. They lose on the commercial.

That is the gap Landed exists to close. Growing brands reach a point where the retail relationship is too big to run off the side of a desk and too important to hand to someone junior, but not yet big enough to justify a senior commercial hire in-house. We are that function, brought in: sales growth and account management across grocery, pharmacy, the independents and food service, delivered as coaching, done with you, or run for you. Food or non-food, the commercial model does not change.

We do one thing. We do not do your legal, your finance, your logistics or your marketing. We are the commercial engine, independent, with nothing else to sell you, so the only thing we are ever steering you toward is a bigger, healthier retail business.

The essays are free and always will be, because understanding how the industry works should not be gated. They are also the clearest way to see how we think before you ever book a call.

Three words sit under everything here, and if you spend any time with Landed you will get tired of hearing them: range, rate of sale, returns. Every buyer conversation you will ever have is about those three things. We would rather you got them right with us than learned them the hard way at a review.

Let's look at your accounts.

A short call, no pitch. We will tell you where the commercial upside is, and whether we are the right people to help you get it.
